Pricing & Quotes

How much does household shifting cost with Super Packers Movers?

The cost of household shifting depends on several factors: distance between source and destination, volume and type of goods, floor access (lift availability), and any special items (piano, large appliances, etc.). We provide free, no-obligation quotes tailored to your specific requirements. Contact us or fill our quote form for an accurate estimate.

Are there any hidden charges?

Absolutely not. Super Packers Movers operates with complete pricing transparency. When we provide you a quote, it includes all costs — packing materials, labour, transportation and delivery. There are no surprise charges at the time of billing. We believe in building long-term trust with our customers.

How can I get a free quote?

You can get a free quote in three easy ways: (1) Fill our online quote form on this website, (2) Call us at +91 9996068007, or (3) WhatsApp us at +91 9996068007. Once you share your move details, our team will provide a comprehensive quote within 30 minutes.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major payment methods including cash, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), NEFT/RTGS bank transfer, and debit/credit cards. Payment terms are discussed and agreed upon before the move begins, with no surprises on moving day.

Packing & Moving

What packing materials do you use?

We use high-quality packing materials including multi-layer bubble wrap, corrugated cardboard boxes, foam padding, stretch film, and waterproof covers. Fragile items like glassware, electronics, and artwork receive special custom packing for maximum protection during transit.

Will your team dismantle and reassemble furniture?

Yes. Our team includes trained professionals who can dismantle beds, wardrobes, modular kitchens, and other furniture at your source address and reassemble them at your new home. This is included in our standard household shifting service.

How long does a typical household move take?

Local moves within the same city typically take 1–2 days including packing and unpacking. Inter-city moves usually take 2–5 days depending on the distance. We provide you with a specific timeline estimate when you book your move, so you can plan accordingly.

Do I need to be present during the move?

It is recommended that you or a trusted representative be present at both the source and destination to supervise the move and guide our team. However, if you cannot be present throughout, we can coordinate with your representative and keep you updated via phone and WhatsApp.

Insurance & Safety

Is my shipment insured during the move?

Yes. We provide transit insurance coverage for all shipments. This protects your goods against damage, loss, or theft during the move. The insurance value is based on the declared value of your goods. Please discuss the insurance options with our team when booking.

What is your guarantee about damage-free delivery?

We proudly maintain 0% damage guarantees across multiple categories: 0% damage due to rain, 0% delayed delivery, 0% pilferage/theft, 0% road damage, 0% breakage in lacing, and 0% damage in handling. These are backed by our insurance coverage and experienced packing team.

What happens if my goods are damaged during transit?

In the unlikely event of any damage, our team will document it with photos and our insurance process will be initiated immediately. We work with reputable insurance partners to ensure quick and fair settlement. Contact us within 24 hours of delivery to report any damage.

Do you provide international relocation services?

Yes. Super Packers Movers provides complete international moving services including customs documentation, air and sea freight, door-to-door delivery worldwide, and transit insurance. We can reach every major destination globally. Contact us for international moving quotes.

Can you handle large-scale corporate or office relocations?

Absolutely. We have specialized corporate relocation teams for office moves of all sizes — from small offices to large corporate campuses. Vehicle Transport

How is my car transported? Is it safe?

We use enclosed carrier trucks for car transport, which fully protect your vehicle from weather, road debris and theft. Each vehicle undergoes a pre-inspection report, is secured with padded straps, and tracked via GPS throughout the journey. Transit insurance is included with every car transport.

How long does car or bike transport take from Delhi to Bangalore?

Car transport from Delhi to Bangalore typically takes 5–8 days depending on the route, road conditions, and type of carrier service selected. We provide you with a delivery window estimate at the time of booking and keep you updated throughout the journey.

Do I need to empty my fuel tank before vehicle transport?

Yes, we recommend keeping the fuel tank at around 1/4 tank (25%) for safety during transport. A full tank adds unnecessary weight and a fully empty tank can cause issues with the fuel pump. Please also remove any valuables and personal items from the vehicle before handover.
